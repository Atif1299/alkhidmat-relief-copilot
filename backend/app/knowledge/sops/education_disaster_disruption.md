# Education — After floods or displacement

**Category:** Education  
**Keywords:** flood, displaced, camp, lost, school closed, learning, classes, baadh, damaged

## Purpose

Keep children learning when floods or displacement interrupt school.

## Rules

1. Children living in camps are referred to Township Learning Support.
2. Books and kits lost in a flood are replaced at priority medium.
3. Requests from one camp are grouped into a single delivery.
4. Record the school the children attended before displacement.
