# Education — Fee and admission support

**Category:** Education  
**Keywords:** fee, fees, admission, dakhla, scholarship, tuition, exam, orphan, yateem, widow, bewa

## Purpose

Route requests for school fees and admissions to the education programme for verification.

## Rules

1. Ask for the school name and a copy of the fee slip.
2. Orphan or widow hardship raises priority to medium.
3. The desk never transfers money; it routes the request to the education programme.
4. Exam or admission deadlines within two weeks are noted on the ticket.
5. Verified cases are matched to the nearest education desk for follow-up.
