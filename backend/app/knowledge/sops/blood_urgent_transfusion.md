# Blood — Urgent transfusion and surgery

**Category:** Blood  
**Keywords:** blood, khoon, transfusion, surgery, operation, accident, bleeding, units, bottle, urgent, hospital  
**Default:** yes

## Purpose

Match urgent blood requests to the nearest blood bank with stock.

## Rules

1. Capture the blood group, number of units and the hospital name.
2. Surgery or transfusion today is priority high.
3. Active bleeding after an accident is priority critical and goes to supervisor approval.
4. Prefer Sundas Foundation Blood Bank (Jail Road) or Alkhidmat Blood Centre (Garden Town) by distance.
5. Never promise units that are not in stock.
