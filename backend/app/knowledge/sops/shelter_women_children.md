# Shelter — Women, children and elderly

**Category:** Shelter  
**Keywords:** women, woman, aurat, khawateen, children, bachay, widow, bewa, elderly, buzurg, pregnant, alone, safety

## Purpose

Keep vulnerable people safe when placing them in shelter.

## Rules

1. Families with young children are placed in a family camp, such as Thokar Family Camp, where possible.
2. Women or children without a male guardian are priority high and the supervisor is informed.
3. Never share a vulnerable family's camp location outside the ticket.
4. Pregnant women and elderly people also get a medical check request.
5. Note any disability so the camp can prepare access.
