# Integrity & HITL Gate SOP

**Category:** Other  
**Keywords:** duplicate, fraud, integrity, hitl, supervisor, risk, phone, cnic

## Purpose

Protect relief inventory from duplicate and high-risk requests.

## Rules

1. Same phone within 24 hours of an open/dispatched case → duplicate flag + HITL.
2. Risk score ≥ 0.75 → escalate to supervisor.
3. Priority **critical** always requires HITL before Matcher/Dispatch.
4. Supervisor approve → continue to Matcher; reject → status rejected, notify requester.
5. Log every escalate/approve/reject in case_events for audit.
