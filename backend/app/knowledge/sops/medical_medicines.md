# Medical — Medicines and supplies

**Category:** Medical  
**Keywords:** medicine, medicines, dawai, dawa, tablets, insulin, prescription, bp, sugar, diabetes, inhaler, asthma, supplies

## Purpose

Handle requests for medicines and medical supplies through a clinic, not as a direct handout.

## Rules

1. Ask for the medicine name or a photo of the prescription.
2. Chronic medicines that are running out, such as insulin, blood pressure or asthma inhalers, are priority high.
3. Prescription-only and controlled medicines are never sent without a doctor's review at the clinic.
4. Route the request through the nearest mobile or field clinic.
5. Record the patient's age and condition on the ticket.
