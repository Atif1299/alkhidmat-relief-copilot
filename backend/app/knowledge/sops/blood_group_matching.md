# Blood — Group matching and donors

**Category:** Blood  
**Keywords:** group, o+, o-, a+, a-, b+, b-, ab+, ab-, negative, positive, donor, rare, type, replacement

## Purpose

Make sure the right blood group is matched and replacement donors are arranged.

## Rules

1. If the blood group is missing, still open the ticket and mark it "type to be confirmed".
2. Rare negative groups are flagged to the supervisor.
3. O negative is used for other groups only on a doctor's instruction.
4. Ask the family to arrange replacement donors through Model Town Donor Camp.
5. Record the patient's name and ward so the bank can release units.
