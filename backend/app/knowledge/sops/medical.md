# Medical Aid SOP — Critical & Routine

**Category:** Medical  
**Keywords:** medical, ambulance, chest, pain, clinic, emergency, first aid, hospital

## Purpose

Route medical requests safely with human approval for life-threatening cases.

## Rules

1. Chest pain, breathlessness, severe bleeding, unconsciousness → priority **critical** → **HITL required**.
2. Do not dispatch ambulance simulation without supervisor approval on critical cases.
3. Match routine medical needs to mobile clinic or first-aid unit by area.
4. Record contact phone; ask for landmark if location vague.
5. After approval, assign Medical-skilled volunteer when available.

## Escalation

Critical medical never auto-dispatches. Supervisor must approve or reject.
