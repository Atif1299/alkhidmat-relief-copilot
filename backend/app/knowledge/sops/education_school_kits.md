# Education — School kits and books

**Category:** Education  
**Keywords:** school, kit, kits, books, kitaab, bag, bastah, uniform, stationery, copies, student, parhai  
**Default:** yes

## Purpose

Send school kit and book requests to the nearest distribution hub.

## Rules

1. Confirm the number of children and their grade.
2. Match School Kit Distribution Hub (Allama Iqbal Town) or the nearest education desk: Township, Gulberg or Johar Town.
3. Default priority is low.
4. The ticket includes the hub contact for pickup.
5. Uniform sizes are recorded per child.
