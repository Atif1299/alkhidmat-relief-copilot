# Food Relief SOP — Alkhidmat Lahore

**Category:** Food  
**Keywords:** food, ration, pack, kitchen, flood, khana, ration, family

## Purpose

Guide desk agents when matching food aid requests to kitchens and depots.

## Rules

1. Confirm household size and location (area within Lahore).
2. Prefer the nearest kitchen/depot with stock > 0.
3. Flood or rain damage → priority at least **medium**; if children under 5 mentioned → **high**.
4. One food pack request per phone per 24 hours unless supervisor overrides.
5. Notify requester with kitchen name and expected pickup/delivery window.

## Matching preference

Township → Alkhidmat Lahore Kitchen; Johar Town → Food Pack Depot; Model Town → Community Kitchen.
