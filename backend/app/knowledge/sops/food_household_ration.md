# Food — Household ration packs

**Category:** Food  
**Keywords:** food, ration, rashan, pack, family, household, khana, khane, atta, groceries, monthly, hungry  
**Default:** yes

## Purpose

Match a household's food request to the nearest kitchen or depot that has stock.

## Rules

1. Confirm household size and the area within Lahore before matching.
2. Match the nearest kitchen or depot with stock above zero: Township → Alkhidmat Lahore Kitchen; Johar Town → Food Pack Depot Johar Town; Model Town → Community Kitchen Model Town; Gulberg → Ration Point Gulberg.
3. A repeat request from the same phone within 48 hours goes to supervisor review, not automatic rejection.
4. Default priority is medium; raise to high when the family reports no food today.
5. Tell the requester the kitchen name and contact on the ticket.
