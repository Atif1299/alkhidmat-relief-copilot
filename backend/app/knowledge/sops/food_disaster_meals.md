# Food — Flood and disaster meals

**Category:** Food  
**Keywords:** flood, baadh, rain, barish, disaster, cooked, meals, stranded, displaced, emergency, sailab

## Purpose

Prioritise cooked meals and food packs for families hit by floods, heavy rain or other disasters.

## Rules

1. Flood or rain damage sets priority to at least medium.
2. Children under five, elderly people or pregnant women in the household raise priority to high.
3. Record the headcount so the kitchen can plan cooked meals, not only dry rations.
4. If the family has also lost its home, the desk opens a shelter request as well.
5. Group requests from one location are handled as one delivery with a single contact person.
