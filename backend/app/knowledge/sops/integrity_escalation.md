# Integrity — Critical and high-risk escalation

**Category:** Integrity  
**Keywords:** critical, escalation, supervisor, approval, risk, hitl, high risk

## Purpose

Make sure a person approves every critical or high-risk case before resources are committed.

## Rules

1. Critical priority always pauses for supervisor approval before matching and dispatch.
2. A risk score of 0.75 or more also pauses for approval.
3. A missing phone number raises the risk score.
4. Approve to resume matching and dispatch; reject to close the case as not approved.
5. Every escalation and decision is logged on the case timeline.
