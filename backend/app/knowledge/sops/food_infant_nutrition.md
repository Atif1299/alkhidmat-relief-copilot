# Food — Infant and special nutrition

**Category:** Food  
**Keywords:** baby, infant, milk, doodh, formula, bachay, bacha, child, newborn, malnutrition, diabetic, special diet

## Purpose

Handle requests for baby milk, infant food and special diets that a standard ration pack does not cover.

## Rules

1. Infant formula or baby milk requests are priority high.
2. Never substitute infant formula with regular milk or a standard ration pack.
3. Record the child's age in months so the right formula stage is sent.
4. Signs of malnutrition, such as weight loss or weakness, also open a medical referral.
5. Diabetic and other special diets are noted on the ticket for the kitchen.
