# Shelter — Winter and rain protection

**Category:** Shelter  
**Keywords:** winter, sardi, cold, thand, blanket, kambal, rain, barish, roof, chhat, leak, tarpaulin, tent

## Purpose

Protect families whose homes are unsafe in cold weather or heavy rain.

## Rules

1. If the home is unsafe to sleep in, place the family in a camp.
2. If only the roof is leaking, record it as a tarpaulin and blanket request at the nearest shelter.
3. Children exposed to cold at night are priority high.
4. Count blankets per person, not per family.
5. Re-check rain-damaged homes before closing the ticket.
