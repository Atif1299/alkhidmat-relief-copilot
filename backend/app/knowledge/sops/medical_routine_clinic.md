# Medical — Routine care and mobile clinics

**Category:** Medical  
**Keywords:** fever, bukhar, clinic, doctor, checkup, cough, khansi, diarrhea, dast, skin, ilaj, sick, beemar

## Purpose

Send non-emergency medical needs to the nearest mobile or field clinic.

## Rules

1. Routine illness is priority medium.
2. Match by area: Johar Town → Alkhidmat Mobile Clinic; Township → Township Field Clinic; Model Town → Model Town Health Desk; Gulberg → Emergency First Aid Unit.
3. A child or elderly person with high fever for three days or more is priority high.
4. If emergency signs appear (chest pain, breathing difficulty), treat the case as critical.
5. Diarrhoea cases after floods are priority high because of dehydration risk.
