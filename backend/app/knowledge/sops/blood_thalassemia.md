# Blood — Thalassemia and regular transfusions

**Category:** Blood  
**Keywords:** thalassemia, thalassaemia, regular, monthly, patient, card, child, haemoglobin, hb, recurring

## Purpose

Support patients who need blood on a regular schedule.

## Rules

1. Record the patient card or registration number.
2. Regular monthly requests are expected; the desk confirms the schedule rather than treating them as fraud.
3. A repeat request inside 48 hours still goes to supervisor review.
4. Priority is medium, or high when the family reports low haemoglobin.
5. Prefer Sundas Foundation Blood Bank, which runs thalassemia care.
