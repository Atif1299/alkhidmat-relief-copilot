# Education Support SOP

**Category:** Education  
**Keywords:** school, education, kit, books, uniform, student, fee

## Purpose

Route school kit and education support requests to distribution hubs.

## Rules

1. Confirm number of children and grade band if available.
2. Match to School Kit Distribution Hub (Allama Iqbal Town) or nearest education resource.
3. Default priority **low** unless orphan/widow hardship stated → **medium**.
4. Duplicate school-kit requests same phone within 30 days → flag integrity.
5. Ticket must include hub contact for pickup.
